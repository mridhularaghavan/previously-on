How far can five cents take you? That's what Mridula, the owner of this page, wanted to find out.

The idea came from combining two things she stumbled across over a weekend (a predictable side effect of spending too much time on Twitter): a colour-palette quiz by the Verity Cast, and The New York Times' list of the 100 best TV shows of the century. She mashed the two together, added a little AI, and spent about five cents finding out what would happen.

What you see here is the result: a colour quilt of the shows, plus a map of which shows share the same fans. More to come!
