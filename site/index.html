A five-cent experiment with Jev, an AI decision model

What colour is Breaking Bad?

I asked an AI to colour-code the 100 best TV shows of the century, as ranked by The New York Times, then predict which of them share fans. The whole experiment cost about five cents.

Why this exists

How far can five cents take you? That's what Mridhula, the owner of this page, wanted to find out.

The idea came from combining two things she stumbled across over a weekend (a predictable side effect of spending too much time on Twitter): a colour-palette quiz by the Verity Cast, and The New York Times' list of the 100 best TV shows of the century. She mashed the two together, added a little AI, and spent about five cents finding out what would happen.

What you see here is the result: a colour quilt of the shows, plus a map of which shows Jev thinks share the same fans. More to come!
